About Me

I am a Research Fellow at the University of Warwick specialising in computational materials physics. My research connects atomistic electronic structure with charge and phonon transport in semiconductors and energy-conversion materials, particularly thermoelectrics and photovoltaics. I investigate how experimentally accessible strategies, including alloying and doping, modify material properties and device performance, using first-principles calculations, transport theory, spectral-function analysis, and drift–diffusion device modelling.

My current work includes carrier-scattering calculations and method development for analysing alloy electronic structures through band unfolding and spectral-function approaches.
